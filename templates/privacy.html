Privacy Policy

Zyviora is a personal/portfolio project, not a commercial product or a licensed healthcare service. This page explains, plainly, what it does with your data.

What's collected

  • Account info: username, email, and full name you provide at signup, plus your password — stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text.
  • Chat messages: what you type in the chat window.
  • Mood, goals, tasks, and reminders: stored in your browser's local storage on your own device, and optionally synced to the server when you log out so they're there next time you log in from that account.

How your messages are handled

Most messages are matched against a fixed set of local rules (greetings, emotional support, productivity tips, and similar) and never leave the server. When a message doesn't match any of those rules, it — along with a short window of recent conversation for context — is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. That means some of what you type is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy, which is outside this project's control.

Not medical care

Zyviora offers supportive, conversational responses — it is not therapy, not a crisis service, and not a substitute for professional mental health care. If a message contains crisis language, the app shows real crisis-line resources (like 988 in the US) alongside its own response, but it cannot reach out to anyone on your behalf.

What isn't done with your data

Nothing here is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build an ad profile. There's no analytics or tracking pixel on this app beyond what's described above.

Your control over it

Mood, goals, tasks, and reminders live in your browser's local storage — clearing your browser's site data for this app removes them from that device. To have your account and its server-side data deleted entirely, contact whoever is operating this instance of the app.

Changes

Since this is a small, evolving project, this policy may change as features change. Check back here if you want the current version.
